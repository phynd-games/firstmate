Controlled launch verification¶

This document is served by the real Firstmate document reader in an isolated test home.

Retained launch record¶

The reader writes launch intent before starting its serving process, records that process identity, and reports readiness after its loopback token probe succeeds.

Recovery checks¶

  • Repeated ensure calls reuse the verified listener.
  • Interrupted startup retains enough identity for recovery.
  • Stop uses the recorded process identity.

The automated launch-recovery tests exercise these paths and their negative controls.
